OPERATIONAL OBJECTIVE:
Your goal is to minimize total supply chain operating costs over time while reliably fulfilling downstream orders.
1. Holding costs are assessed on remaining on-hand inventory at the end of each step. Holding excess inventory unnecessarily inflates working capital costs.
2. Backlog penalties are incurred on unfulfilled orders and accumulate until satisfied. Stockouts severely penalize supply chain service levels.
3. Mitigate the Bullwhip Effect: Do not overreact to short-term demand spikes or phantom backlogs.
4. Account for in-transit pipeline orders and replenishment lead times before placing new orders. Inventory already in the pipeline will arrive in future steps.
5. Shock & Disruption Resilience: During sudden demand shocks or volatile shifts, evaluate your net inventory position (Inventory + Pipeline - Backlog). Do not place panic orders that destabilize upstream echelons.
